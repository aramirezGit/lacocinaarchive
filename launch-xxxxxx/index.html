As an inaugural gesture, we intervened the interior architecture of Goleb’s project space. We emptied the room, removed the surface of its walls, floor and ceiling, with the intention to unravel its structure. The empty space is an invitation to explore different kinds of discursive scenarios. A space to be imagined as a possibility.
